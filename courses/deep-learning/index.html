    About Deep Learning Zoomcamp

    Deep Learning is the engine behind all the Generative AI advances that we see around us today. This course will unpack deep learning, developing its building blocks from scratch. You will learn the basics of deep neural networks - layers and activations - and how to set up and train them. You will learn about special purpose networks in the field of computer vision to process images and videos (convolutional networks) and networks in the field of natural language processing to process text and sequences. YOu will also learn to write Python programs (using TensorFlow and Keras libraries) to create deep learning models and train them on real-world datasets.

    • You will master Reflection, a design pattern that powers agentic AI systems

    👥 Who Should Join?

    This course is for people who learn by doing. After completing it, you'll have a working codebase and the hands-on experience to build your own LLM-powered applications.

    • Software Engineers: Developers who want to apply AI techniques to build autonomous systems that handle multi-step workflows.
    • Professionals: Professionals with intermediate-level Python programming skills to be able to follow along with the implementations.
    • Practitioners: Professionals with a basic understanding of large language models and APIs who want to deepen their practical skills.
